Questions for Discussion - MOU Module

Q1: Who can submit MOU entries?

Answer Received: Any faculty can submit (exactly like publications).

Q2: Do MOUs need approval workflow?

Answer Received: Yes, it needs MOU approval like publication normal.

Q3: Should MOUs contribute to faculty scoring?

Answer Received: Yes, for now we will keep it open because this is new and there is no score for that. For now, it is counted under bonus.

Q4: What evidence files are required?

Answer Received: They can submit either link (like usual) or they can upload files. In every evidence, they need to provide: a descriptive box (can mention some words), link (definitely needed), and they can upload some files (word file, PDF, zip file, whatever). This is for the MOU model.

Questions for Discussion - Adjunct Professor Module (Agent-Professor)

Q1: Who manages agent-professor records?

Answer Received: The research coordinator can do, the dean can do, research director can do, and of course, we can assign someone to coordinate agent-professor records. As for now, for example, we have agent-professor coordinator, so we will assign one of the faculties to do that.

Q2: How should agent-professors be linked to faculty?

Answer Received: There is no direct link, they just need to refer. For example, this faculty will fill the application referring, saying that this faculty is proposed. So, any faculty can propose saying that agent faculty X to be added, then it will go to the research director, research director will approve, the dean will approve, and then it goes to the research coordinator will approve, and then the dean will approve, and then it goes to the research director for final approval.

Q3: Should agent collaborations count in scoring?

Answer Received: Yes, keep it open, it is under bonus. How much? There is no score for now. In the policy, it's not mentioned, so we will discuss this with them once open, but definitely there will be scoring. Keep it open under bonus dynamically, maybe 5 points, 10 points, whatever.

Q4: How to link agent to publications?

Answer Received: We need the records of the agent faculties alone, right? And that will be later on accounted with the school publications. So, for example, school of IT have published, for example, 40 papers, okay? 30 of them are from the faculty themselves and 20 from agent faculties, right? So, we need to know how many agent, how many academic, and how many is fellowship. So, in every school, you have three types. You have the normal faculty members who teach, you have fellowships who are under the faculty school just to do publications, and you have the agent professors. So, that is the three types that need to be counted for every school, okay?

Questions for Discussion - TRN Course Details & Types

Q1: What course information should be captured?

Answer Received: As per the Excel sent. They need to fill the information as per the Excel.

Q2: Who assigns RTN types to courses?

Answer Received: The faculty himself, because he will be adding it to his course. He will choose his course, he will choose which type of RTN that he is adding.

Q3: Are there more RTN types beyond RTN-3 and RTN-4?

Answer Received: No, only RTN 3, RTN 4 for now, but it is dynamic that we can introduce this to level 2, level 1. That's it. This is now for level 3, level 4. Maybe we can introduce it to level 2, level 4. So, later on, they can decide, right? Again, it should be dynamic where the research coordinator, or the research director assigning the policy at the beginning, they need to choose this, like choosing this course also.

Q4: What evidence is required for RTN course submissions?

Answer Received: Again, any evidence is either link, description, or upload file.

Q5: How should RTN course data be imported from Excel?

Answer Received: The same format, what I have sent you, need to be filled. So, the faculty will fill the information, can immediately upload it. But usually, the faculty member have one course, two course, not more than that. So, you don't need Excel to upload. Who does this? Now, it goes like adjunct professors. There is a coordinator for RTN, okay? For now, this is how we are doing it. There is an adjunct faculty for RTN. He is supposed to upload all this information to one Excel. In option 1, it says that the research coordinator and his faculty member can assign one faculty member to do the RTN through other professors, so he can get the information from them and then upload one Excel sheet directly to the system. The possibility where the faculty members themselves can add the RTN to the system directly. So, both scenarios should work, okay?
